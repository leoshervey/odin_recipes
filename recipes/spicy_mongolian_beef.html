Spicy Mongolian Chicken
Description
Serves 6

A simple but spicy stir fried dish with beef, carrots, and green onions. Serve over rice for a satisfying meal.

Nutritional Information (per serving)
Calories Carbs Protein Fat
155kcal 9g 11g 9g
Ingredients
  • 1 teaspoon sesame seeds
  • 3 tablespoons soy sauce, divided
  • 1 tablespoon plus 2 ½ teaspoons cornstarch, divided
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 pound beef round steak, cut into thin strips
  • ¾ cup water
  • ½ teaspoon white sugar
  • 1 teaspoon red pepper flakes, or to taste
  • 2 tablespoons vegetable oil, divided
  • 2 carrots, thinly sliced
  • 1 bunch green onions, cut into 2-inch pieces
Method
  1. Toast sesame seeds in a dry skillet over medium heat until golden, 1 to 2 minutes; set aside.
  2. Mix 1 tablespoon soy sauce, 1 tablespoon cornstarch, and garlic in a medium bowl. Add beef strips and toss to coat. Let stand for at least 10 minutes.
  3. Mix water, remaining 2 tablespoons soy sauce, remaining 2 ½ teaspoons cornstarch, sugar, toasted sesame seeds, and red pepper flakes in a small bowl; set aside.
  4. Heat 1 tablespoon oil in a wok or large skillet over high heat. Add beef and cook, stirring, until browned, about 1 minute; transfer to a plate.
  5. Heat remaining 1 tablespoon oil in the same pan. Add carrots and white parts of green onions; stir-fry for 2 minutes. Add green parts of onions and stir-fry for 1 minute.
  6. Stir the sauce mixture again, then add it to the pan with the beef. Cook, stirring, until the sauce boils and thickens and the beef is cooked through, 1 to 2 minutes.
